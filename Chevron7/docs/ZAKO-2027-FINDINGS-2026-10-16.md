# ZaKo from 1. 1. 2027: findings (2026-10-16)

Research only. Nothing in the app changes until the owner decides the open points at the end.
Every statement below names its source; quotes are Slovak originals, the rest is English.

## Summary

- From **1. 1. 2027** (zákon č. 325/2022 Z. z., Čl. I body 73 až 82, postponed by 237/2024 and
  239/2025) the order of a paper to electronic (P→E) guaranteed conversion reverses: the record
  is created and sent to the central register (CEZZK) **first**, the clause is then built "z údajov
  získaných z centrálnej evidencie záznamov", and only then are the clause and the new document
  signed together. Chevron7 today signs the client ASiC-E first and sends the record afterwards.
- The law has **no transitional provision**. The implementing decree for 2027 is still a draft:
  **LP/2026/337**, "Návrh vyhlášky ... o zaručenej konverzii", which replaces 70/2021 Z. z. on
  1. 1. 2027. It closed public comment on 2026-07-03 and has been in "Vyhodnotenie
  pripomienkového konania" since 2026-07-04. It is not published in the Zbierka.
- In the draft, CEZZK itself creates the record's identifier when it records the record (§ 9), the
  clause carries **only that identifier** and the declaration, and a record CEZZK does not take
  means **no clause data and no conversion** (§ 7 ods. 6). Records made under the old rules and
  not yet sent can still be sent under the old rules (§ 12 ods. 1).
- The register behind it is a **new system, EZZK 2.0** (isvs_14693, government cloud, built
  2026 to 2027 with EU money; SLA from 01/2027). Its API is **not published**. The live EZZK SOAP
  contract is unchanged since the 2026-09-17 snapshot and has no operation that returns "data for
  the clause".
- Forms: the 2027 record is `50349287.ConversionRecordOfPaperToElectronicDocument_2021.sk` 1.2
  (published 2022, MIRRI says "účinná od 1.1.2027"). **No clause form for 2027 is published.**
  Under the three-month rule (70/2021 § 12 ods. 2, draft § 11 ods. 2), a form first published
  after 1. 10. 2026 cannot apply on 1. 1. 2027; on 2026-10-16 the module held no new clause form,
  only the dormant `_2021.sk` 1.4 of 2021.
- Conclusion: the 2027 flow cannot be built to a published contract today. What can be built now
  is the date switch, the record 1.2 renderer and a record-first ordering on today's SOAP; what
  EZZK 2.0 will actually return, and which clause form applies, needs MIRRI.

The postponements matter: the same change was first due on 1. 10. 2024, then 1. 10. 2025, now
1. 1. 2027. A fourth postponement is possible, so whatever ships must switch on a date that can be
moved without a code change in the flow itself.

There is no § 36a. The law's transitional provisions end with § 60k (2026, unrelated); nothing
covers a conversion in progress at midnight. The regime follows the time the conversion is
performed.

## 2. Order of steps

### Today (§ 36 ods. 3 and ods. 5 until 31. 12. 2026) and Chevron7

Law: request the evidence number before starting (ods. 5); transform; create the clause; sign
clause and document together with a timestamp; create the record.

Chevron7 (`ZakoSessionStore.authorizeAndSign`, `Sources/Chevron7App/ZakoSessionStore.swift:1324`):

1. Allocate the evidence number after the card and its mandate certificate (`fetchEvidenceNumber`).
2. Read EZZK server time; it becomes `ConversionExecutionDateTime` and must fall on the number's
   Bratislava day (`EZZKEvidenceNumberPolicy.isUsable`).
3. PDF/A, fingerprint, clause 1.3 XDC (`ZakoClauseDeliveryBuilder`), record 1.0 rendered and
   validated (`ZakoRecordDeliveryBuilder`).
4. Sign the client ASiC-E (PDF/A + clause), number leaves `EvidenceNumberPool`.
5. Register row `.signed`, sign the record XDC (second signature).
6. `EZZKSubmissionCoordinator` / `EZZKStatusChecker` send it with `ReceiveConversionRecord`
   and look it up until processed.

### From 1. 1. 2027 (§ 36 ods. 3 in the new version)

> a) transformuje pôvodný dokument v listinnej podobe na elektronický dokument,
> b) vytvorí záznam o vykonanej zaručenej konverzii a zašle ho do centrálnej evidencie záznamov,
> c) z údajov získaných z centrálnej evidencie záznamov vytvorí osvedčovaciu doložku vo forme elektronického dokumentu,
> d) autorizuje osvedčovaciu doložku a novovzniknutý elektronický dokument spoločne a pripojí časovú pečiatku.

§ 36 ods. 5 (number before starting) is deleted. The draft decree:

- § 7 ods. 3: a record is recorded in CEZZK (a) if it is authorized (QES with the mandate
  certificate or a qualified seal, with a qualified timestamp, § 7 ods. 4), or (b) after the
  person's authentication without a signature. § 12 ods. 2: way (b) is available "do 1. januára
  2028". In 2027 the record is still signed.
- § 7 ods. 6: "Ak záznam nie je do centrálnej evidencie zaznamenaný, osoba vykonávajúca konverziu
  je o tom bezodkladne informovaná a nezíska údaje na vytvorenie osvedčovacej doložky."
- § 9: CEZZK creates and provides the clause data automatically; one identifier per record, never
  reused, linked to the person who recorded it, "v slovnej, číselnej alebo kombinovanej forme,
  ako aj vo forme grafickej".
- Explanatory report (K § 7 až 9): "Týmto údajom bude jednoznačný identifikátor záznamu danej
  zaručenej konverzie a okrem neho nebude doložka obsahovať žiadne iné údaje o zaručenej
  konverzii." And: the new document comes into existence only once the record is correctly
  recorded in CEZZK.
- The draft has **no number allocation and no 24-hour rules**: 70/2021 § 9 ods. 2 and ods. 3
  písm. e) and § 10 have no counterpart. But the 2027 law still names "podrobnosti o spôsobe
  vyžiadania a o prideľovaní evidenčného čísla ... a údajov na vytvorenie osvedčovacej doložky"
  (§ 59 ods. 1 písm. h) bod 4), and record form 1.2 still has a mandatory
  `ConversionRecordEvidenceNumber`. Whether a number is still requested before the record, or the
  identifier is assigned on receipt, is open (question M2).

### What Chevron7's flow becomes (P→E, card)

1. Card and mandate certificate check (unchanged).
2. EZZK server time becomes the conversion time and selects the regime (section 6).
3. PDF/A and its fingerprint (unchanged; the record 1.2 fingerprint is hex).
4. Render record 1.2 with the new document's name, type, format, fingerprint, the person and the
   conversion time. If a number is still allocated first, it goes in here.
5. **Sign the record** (first signature, today the second one).
6. **Send it and wait** until CEZZK has recorded it and returned the identifier. On EZZK 1.0 SOAP
   today that means `ReceiveConversionRecord` code 0 ("accepted for processing") and then a lookup
   until code 0 ("processed"), observed up to 18 minutes on test (`EZZK-INTEGRATION.md`,
   "Evidence numbers"). The client is waiting at the advocate's desk during this.
7. Build the clause from the returned data.
8. **Sign the client ASiC-E** (PDF/A + clause) with a qualified timestamp (second signature).
9. Deliver and register.

Consequences:

- A refused record, an EZZK outage or a lost reply now stops the conversion **before the client
  gets anything**. Today the client output exists and only the register row goes `.rejected`,
  `.late` or `.outcomeUnknown`. `EZZKSubmissionCoordinator`'s "never resend blindly" rules still
  apply, but inside the ZaKo screen rather than in the background checker.
- The record and the client container are signed in reverse order; both still need a PIN or the
  eID BOK. The record's `NewDocumentName` and fingerprint must already describe the PDF/A that
  goes into the container, so the PDF/A cannot change between steps 3 and 8.
- The Supreme Court asked in the comment procedure for an offline fallback (client-generated
  identifier, record sent later) because CEZZK becomes a single point of failure; the
  Slovak Post, the Supreme Court and others commented on § 7 and § 12. The evaluation is not
  published, so the final text may still change on exactly these points.

## 3. EZZK API

- Live WSDL and every XSD part of `EZZKService.svc` and `LogInService.svc`, fetched 2026-10-16
  from `ezzk.iomo.sk` and `ezzk-test.iomo.sk`, are identical to
  `docs/reference/ezzk-soap/2026-09-17/` once `location`/`schemaLocation` are normalised. Parts
  `wsdl2`, `wsdl3` and `xsd8` onwards do not exist (the service returns its HTML help page).
- Operations: production `GetOptions`, `GetConversionRecordEvidenceNumber`,
  `ConsumeConversionRecordEvidenceNumber`, `ReceiveConversionRecord`, `GetConversionRecord`,
  `GetConversionRecordInformationPurpose`; test adds `GetConversionRecord2`, whose `ZiadostVypis2`
  and `OdpovedVypis2` extend the 1.0 types with an empty sequence. Nothing returns clause data.
- The draft's IT impact analysis (LP/2026/337, "4b_analýza vplyvov na informatizáciu") lists a
  new system, "Centrálny systém evidencie záznamov o vykonanej zaručenej konverzii 2.0 (EZZK 2.0)",
  `isvs_14693`, in the government cloud, with data provided "prostredníctvom API rozhrania". The
  budget analysis plans 1 150 000 EUR of investment in 2026 and 1 002 837 EUR in 2027, SLA from
  01/2027, funded from Program Slovensko call PSK-MIRRI-622-2024-DV-EFRR. Draft § 8 ods. 8: the
  API and its description are published "spolu s používateľským rozhraním centrálnej evidencie".
- MIRRI's CEZZK page still links only integration manual v1.4 (2019). The test environment for
  third parties (`ezzk-test.iomo.sk`) is the current one.
- `ReceiveConversionRecord` is form-agnostic: the record is an `ATTACHMENT` ASiC-E and EZZK reads
  the form type from the record's own schema (manual v1.4). So a record 1.2 could in principle be
  sent through today's SOAP. Whether EZZK 1.0 accepts a 1.2 record, before or after 1. 1. 2027,
  is untested (pre-check M4).

Notes:

- `Position` is gone from the 1.2 form although the 2027 law § 37 ods. 1 still lists "funkcia a
  pracovné zaradenie" among the record's personal data. Flag to MIRRI (M3).
- Chevron7's security element catalogue (`SecurityElementCatalogue`, 16 kinds: binding cord,
  sealing tape, wax seal, material protections and others) has to map onto the 13 enum values,
  with "iný manuálny vstup" plus `...DescriptionOther` for the rest. The location must come from
  the enum; the analysis canvas knows each element's box, so a location can be derived and shown
  for confirmation. The Supreme Court asked in the comments for a ministry codelist of security
  elements over the API; the outcome is open.
- `ZakoCodelists` today writes numeric codelist codes (`12`, `15`, `11`, `14`, `53`, `4001`).

- The engine signs a record XDC because ZaKo sets `SigningRequest.signsAsRecordContainer`, not by

- Forms in `docs/reference/forms/` are guarded by `embed-official-forms.sh` and
  `OfficialFormTests`; record 1.2 is copied there only in step 2, with its own provenance entry.

## 5. Clause form: the open problem

- 2027 law § 37 ods. 2: the clause holds "údaje identifikujúce záznam ... v centrálnej evidencii
  záznamov a vyhlásenie osoby, ktorá konverziu vykonala, o dodržaní postupu podľa tohto zákona".
- Draft § 5 ods. 1: the clause's model "je určený elektronickým formulárom" in the forms module;
  the draft has no annex with clause content any more.
- Published today:
  - Clause `50349287.ConversionCertificateOfPaperToElectronicDocument.sk` 1.3 (Chevron7's current
    target), "účinná od 01.12.2019", no end date. It carries the fingerprint, the original
    document details, security elements and the performing person: far more than the 2027 clause.
  - Clause `50349287.ConversionCertificateOfPaperToElectronicDocument_2021.sk` 1.4 (zip sha256
    `93e06e27708be2db5dcffca3da973531b5e2477d031f479779d9363071133371`, schema sha256
    `a17a74ba1f11f1c057515cea4b677b615e2d2e1865b0467151c96f4845aaf7d4`): root documentation
    "Tento dokument vznikol zarucenou konverziou ... Vsetky ostatne podrobne informacie sa
    nachadzaju v zazname o zarucenej konverzii v centralnej evidencii na webovej stranke
    http://ezzk.iomo.sk pod evidencnym cislom". Holds the evidence number, conversion time,
    `LegalSubject` with mandatory ID, original documents with sheets, non-empty pages and security
    elements (enums), and no fingerprint and no person name. It was built for vyhláška 70/2021
    Príloha č. 3, not for the draft's "identifier only" clause, and its `meta.xml` says
    `inForceFrom` 2021-06-01 and `inForceTo` 2021-05-31: it was never in force.
  - MIRRI's CEZZK page lists "version 1.2" clauses, but those links open the
    `ConversionRecord..._2021.sk` 1.2 **record** pages.
- Draft § 11 ods. 2 (as 70/2021 § 12 ods. 2): a form applies "najskôr tri mesiace odo dňa, kedy je
  elektronický formulár prístupný v module elektronických formulárov". For 1. 1. 2027 that means
  published by 1. 10. 2026. Nothing new was in the dataset on 2026-10-16, so only clause 1.3 and
  the dormant `_2021.sk` 1.4 (accessible since 2021, so MIRRI could make it effective by changing
  its dates) can apply on 1. 1. 2027. Even 1.4 does not match the draft's identifier-only clause:
  it still carries the conversion time, the legal subject, sheets and security elements.

## 6. Midnight of 31. 12. 2026

- Which time decides: the conversion's own time, `ConversionExecutionDateTime`, which Chevron7
  already takes from EZZK's server time (`ezzkService.serverTime()`), never from the Mac's clock.
  The switch is `2027-01-01T00:00:00` Europe/Bratislava, which is `2026-12-31T23:00:00Z` (CET,
  UTC+01:00).
- Mechanism already in the code: `FormPackRepository.pack(for:at:policy:)` picks the one pack
  active at the conversion time (`ConversionFormPack.effectiveFrom` / `effectiveUntil`), and every
  register row stores its `FormPackStamp`. Today there is one pack,
  `autogram-p2e-legacy-swift-1.0`, from 1970 with no end. Step 2 can give it an end of
  2026-12-31T23:00:00Z and add a 2027 pack, so one value moves the switch if the date is
  postponed again.
- Chevron7's conversion is one call (`authorizeAndSign`): the server time is read once, before
  anything is rendered or signed, so a conversion is entirely in one regime. A number allocated
  at 23:59 on 31. 12. and used after midnight is already refused by the same-day rule
  (`EZZKError.evidenceNumberExpired`).
- Rows signed in 2026 and still unsent at midnight (`.signed`, `.queuedForSubmission`, `.late`,
  `.outcomeUnknown`) carry record 1.0 under the old rules. Draft § 12 ods. 1: "zabezpečí sa, aby
  ich bolo možné do centrálnej evidencie zapísať; na záznam a jeho zápis sa použijú doterajšie
  predpisy". So `EZZKStatusChecker` keeps sending and looking them up unchanged, provided EZZK 1.0
  SOAP (or whatever EZZK 2.0 offers for old records) still answers in 2027 (question M1).
- A conversion that starts on 31. 12. and reaches "Autorizovať" after midnight simply takes the
  2027 regime; the form fields the advocate filled are the same data, so nothing is lost, but the
  2027 path needs the record 1.2 fields (`NewDocumentType`, enum security elements) to be
  collectable before then.

## 7. What stays

- The SOAP transport, login, Keychain handling, certificate pin and `EZZKProductionPolicy`, at
  least until EZZK 2.0 publishes its API.
- `EZZKSubmissionCoordinator` states and the "look up before resend" rules; in 2027 they also run
  inside the ZaKo flow.
- Mandate certificate requirement, qualified timestamp for both signatures
  (`TimestampAuthority.zakoQualifiedURLs`), PDF/A-2b output, ASiC-E packaging of PDF/A and clause.
- The register as the advocate's own record, although the law no longer requires a local register
  (2027 § 39; draft explanatory report: "upúšťa od povinnosti vedenia lokálnej evidencie").

## 8. Questions for MIRRI (only the owner can send them)

- M2. EZZK 2.0: when are the API description and a test environment published? Is an evidence

- M4 is not a question for MIRRI: whether test EZZK accepts a record 1.2 through
  `ReceiveConversionRecord` today is a step-2 pre-check Chevron7 runs itself with a test number

## 9. Decisions for the owner

1. **What step 2 builds before MIRRI answers.** (a) The date switch, the record 1.2 renderer and a
   record-first flow on today's SOAP (number, sign record, send, wait for code 0, clause with that
   number, sign client container), ready to adapt when EZZK 2.0 is published; or (b) the date switch
   only, with ZaKo outside Demo refused from 1. 1. 2027 until the 2027 flow is verified against
   EZZK; or (c) both: build (a) and keep (b) as a switch the owner can turn on if EZZK 2.0 is not
   ready. Recommendation: (c).
2. **Clause in 2027.** Keep clause 1.3 (in force, with no end date) with the
   number from CEZZK, or switch to `_2021.sk` 1.4. Recommendation: 1.3 until MIRRI names another
   form, with the clause form chosen by the form pack so a later switch is one entry.
3. **When the clause may be built.** On `ReceiveConversionRecord` code 0, or only after a lookup
   says processed (code 0), which took up to 18 minutes on test. Recommendation: wait for
   processed, with a visible progress state and a cancel that keeps the row for the background
   checker, because draft § 7 ods. 6 ties the clause to the record actually being recorded.
4. **Contact MIRRI now** with M1 to M3 (and ask for the evaluated text of LP/2026/337).

## Owner's decision (2026-10-16)

Option (c): Chevron7 builds the date switch, record 1.2 and the record-first flow on today's
EZZK SOAP, and keeps a switch that refuses ZaKo outside Demo from 1. 1. 2027 in case EZZK 2.0 is
not ready. Clause 1.3 stays; the clause is built only after EZZK reports the record processed.
